04 — Background Full about →

I started with a strong curiosity about computers and spent years using, configuring, and troubleshooting Linux and Unix-like environments. Over time, that curiosity shifted from simply using systems to understanding how they work below the application layer: operating systems, processes, memory management, concurrency, and performant systems.

I am now deliberately strengthening my foundations in C, algorithms, operating systems, Linux, and machine learning. I am especially interested in the intersection of low-level systems and intelligent software, and I am actively working toward research-oriented internships and open-source contributions.
