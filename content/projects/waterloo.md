## Overview

**Waterloo** is a prototype video tracking utility designed to convert disparate educational YouTube videos into a structured, trackable course queue with saved progress timestamps.

---

## Why I Built It

YouTube's recommendation algorithms actively distract from self-paced academic lectures. Waterloo was an experiment in building a focused, distraction-free environment that preserves viewing progress across technical playlists and video chapters.

---

## What I Learned & Engineering Takeaways

- Working with embedded media APIs and tracking video timecode events.
- Structuring course queues with localStorage persistence.
