## Overview

**Versity** is an exploratory web client built around the SRM Academia student portal. It aimed to provide a faster, distraction-free interface for timetable scheduling, attendance tracking, and course details.

---

## Why I Built It

The default university student portal can be slow and cluttered with redundant navigation. Versity was conceived to create a cleaner, utility-first client tailored specifically to student daily workflows.

---

## What I Learned & Engineering Takeaways

- Handling session-based authentication cookies and navigating stateful university portal endpoints.
- Parsing structured HTML tables into standardized JSON schemas for timetable display.
